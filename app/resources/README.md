# Offline diagnosis catalogue

`icd_diagnoses_ru_en.tsv.gz` is generated by `tools/build_icd_catalog.py`.
It contains codeable categories with Russian and English titles and is loaded
locally; the application does not send patient searches to an internet API.

Sources:

- WHO ICD-11 MMS 2026-01 Russian simple tabulation (the source also contains
  the matching English title):
  `https://icdcdn.who.int/static/releasefiles/2026-01/SimpleTabulation-ICD-11-MMS-ru.zip`
- WHO ICD-10 2019 English expanded metadata:
  `https://icdcdn.who.int/icd10/meta/icd102019enMeta.zip`
- Russian ICD-10 machine-readable export, based on the Russian Ministry of
  Health classifier: `https://10-mkb.ru/assets/downloads/mkb10_database.csv`

ICD-10 national and WHO releases are not identical. The generated catalogue
keeps the union of codes; if one source has no matching translation, the UI
falls back to the available title instead of omitting the diagnosis.
